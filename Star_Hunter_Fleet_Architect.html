Fleet strategy engine

Three doctrines; objective-based composition from owned deployable blueprints. Recommendations are heuristic, not battle simulations.
Military-port capacity and single-fleet command limit are separate. Reinforcements, flagship effects, special modules, repairs and exact aircraft bays are not modeled. The planner will never count fighters or corvettes as independent fleet ships.

Ship-by-ship upgrade plans

Your active fleet is shown first. Strategies influence which systems to enhance. Exact in-game node names and TP costs require validation in each blueprint screen.

Upgrade points can be applied while you continue farming combat experience; spending earned TP does not reduce the EXP your ships can gain. The stages are priorities, not fabricated exact TP prices.

How to use Fleet Architect

01 · Select actual blueprints

Tick only unlocked variants. A visible but locked alternate variant should not be selected. Your choices are saved in this browser automatically.

02 · Verify the game values

Use the current blueprint screen to check CP cost, default row, permanent + EXP TP and ship limit. Some older sources use different numbers.

03 · Choose a doctrine

Offensive boosts damage and heavy-target focus. Balanced spreads roles. Defensive emphasizes tanks, repairs and anti-aircraft support when available.

04 · Generate your fleet

The planner suggests ship counts within your selected per-fleet capacity. Its model is heuristic. Always confirm production availability and any special row modifications.

05 · Check aircraft logistics

Owning fighters or corvettes does not mean you can deploy them. You need a compatible ship with available hangar space, and capacity differs by system and modules.

06 · Share with your squad

Use Export to back up or transfer your setup. Copy a fleet summary, or print a strategy sheet. Import accepts Fleet Architect JSON backups.
