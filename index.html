FIELD GUIDE / 02

First-day priorities

Your early progress depends much more on resource flow and base expansion than on finding rare ships.

THE RULE OF FOUR
Before logging off: miners working · construction queued · research active · fleet safe.

Your first sessions

1
Finish the tutorial and claim every main mission reward.

Main missions pay for early expansion. Follow them instead of guessing which building is next.

2
Build utility ships as soon as the dock is available.

Find reachable metal, crystal and deuterium resource belts. Launch mining immediately.

3
Upgrade buildings required for the next base expansion.

Use the expansion requirements screen. Do not max unrelated buildings just because you can.

4
Build a small, survivable combat fleet.

Complete manageable bounty and privateer missions. Avoid expensive fights you may lose.

5
Join your assigned squad and say hello.

Report your UTC time zone, normal active hours and where your base is located.

Three-day starter targets

DAY 1

Finish tutorials. Start mining and the expansion chain.

DAY 2

Increase utility capacity, complete missions and improve blueprints.

DAY 3

Coordinate relocation, test your core fleet, join squad objectives.

DO TODAY

  • Start or extend mining
  • Claim mission rewards
  • Queue the next expansion requirement
  • Check research / blueprints
  • Read squad messages

DON'T DO YET

  • Fight powerful privateers alone
  • Attack other players without orders
  • Move your base without coordination
  • Spend every speedup instantly
  • Invest heavily in throwaway ships

Mining & resources

Build reliable income first. Strong fleets are useless when you cannot replace losses.

Your economy is your engine

METAL

Most frequent expansion and shipbuilding bottleneck.

CRYSTAL

Important for ships and advanced construction.

DEUTERIUM

Needed for some advanced ships and structures.

1Keep every utility ship working

Idle utility CP means lost income. Replace empty nodes promptly.

2Shorten travel time

A nearby node can outperform a rich distant one once travel is considered.

3Upgrade the mining UAV first

Mining speed is usually the best early enhancement; cargo/warp gains depend on route length.

4Use larger miners when unlocked

Medium utility ships arrive through Zone 4 infrastructure; large utility ships later.

Fleets & combat

A balanced fleet is often more effective than a random assortment of higher-cost ships.

How to build a sensible combat fleet

FRONT ROWTanks / evasive defenders

Absorb direct fire. Prioritize evasion, armor and HP where applicable.

MIDDLE ROWProtected anti-ship damage

Missiles, torpedoes, and flexible support depend on the blueprint.

BACK ROWProtected damage / carriers

UAV ships, some cruisers, and logistics. Not invulnerable to aircraft.

COMMAND POINTS ARE NOT ALL THE SAME
Military port CP = total warships you can own. Fleet CP = how many you can put into a normal fleet. Reinforcement slots and aircraft capacity are separate rules.

Five fleet-building rules

  1. Choose the best blueprints and variants you actually own, not a universal tier list.
  2. Protect valuable damage dealers with enough frontline survivability.
  3. Check a ship's actual row assignment; you cannot freely drag it between rows.
  4. Do not confuse anti-aircraft damage stats with reliable fleet-wide AA interception.
  5. Review battle reports: which row died, what did damage, and who was targeted?

Example: evasive destroyers up front, missile damage in the middle and UAV/torpedo ships in back. Composition must match your own blueprints.

After each major fight: compare losses, damage taken and target priority. If your frontline dies too early, reduce damage investment in favor of survivability.

FIELD GUIDE / 06

Blueprints, TP & experience

Upgrade your core ships, but understand which investments survive the season.

What the technology numbers mean

PERMANENT TP

Usually gained through research or technology files. Retained across star systems and assigned to a suitable blueprint.

COMBAT EXP TP

Earned from battles and microchips. Temporary progression that normally resets when starting a new star-system campaign.

Best beginner enhancement habits

  1. Spend earned enhancement TP: no EXP bonus comes from leaving upgrades unused.
  2. Upgrade the ship's job: tank = survival; damage dealer = weapons; miner = efficiency.
  3. Farm safe privateers: early combat EXP levels your main combat blueprints.
  4. Save combat microchips intelligently: NPC EXP has a lower progression cap than the overall EXP-TP cap.
  5. Be careful assigning permanent class TP: moving points between different models may require restoration.
Season reminder: Your constructed base and fleets are rebuilt in a new campaign. Permanent blueprints and their eligible permanent TP persist. Do not confuse permanent research progression with temporary combat levels.

FIELD GUIDE / 07

Squad & community rules

Individual growth matters, but coordinated participation is what wins star systems.

How to be useful to the community from day one

1
Report your time zone and daily active hours.

Example: UTC+2 · usually online 18:00–22:00. Use a consistent time reference.

2
Read squad mail and scheduled objectives.

Ask your captain which city, sector or enemy fleet is the priority.

3
Relocate only to an agreed position.

Unplanned moves can obstruct allied mining, block territory routes or isolate your base.

4
Use operations carefully.

Place operation circles near specific objectives; respect shared mining and planned rally areas.

5
Prepare before joining attacks.

Repair ships, check fleet composition and tell leadership when you are ready.

6
Communicate when inactive.

Short notice is better than disappearing during a coordinated operation.

New in 2026: squad and community markers can carry schedules, making it easier to rally around one current priority. Check the event/schedule attached to leadership markers.

NEW MEMBER MESSAGE

“Joined squad. UTC+__ . Active __:__–__:__. Base at ____. Current expansion zone ____. Ready for instructions.”

LEADERSHIP SUPPORT

Assign each recruit a contact person, nearby mining area and next squad objective. New players need clear tasks, not vague “be active” demands.

Mistakes & daily checklist

Save this page and use it before every logout.

The 10 mistakes to avoid

01

Letting utility ships sit idle.

02

Maxing optional buildings before expansion prerequisites.

03

Building ships just to use all port CP.

04

Attacking higher-level privateers without checking losses.

05

Ignoring technology points on miners and core combat ships.

06

Using aircraft blueprints without a compatible carrier.

07

Assuming a high AA stat automatically protects the fleet.

08

Relocating or attacking without squad coordination.

09

Spending rare speedups or premium currency without a plan.

10

Forgetting that campaign assets and permanent progression differ.

Daily 5-minute check-in

□ Every miner is active and has a viable resource node.

□ The next base upgrade / required facility is queued.

□ Main quests, research and free rewards have been checked.

□ Ships are repaired and main fleet TP are invested.

□ Squad mail, rally time and leader instructions have been read.

Weekly progress check

Share your expansion zone, mining readiness, strongest fleet, usual play hours, and any help you need. Leadership can make better assignments when progress is visible.
