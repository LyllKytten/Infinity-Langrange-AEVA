MISSION CONTROL

How it works

Everything here is editable. No external service, account or subscription is needed to use this website.

01

Build your inventory

Open My Blueprints. Mark owned variants, set the maximum number of ships available, then verify each ship’s row and command points against the game.

02

Choose a doctrine

Set the fleet CP limit, combat strategy and aircraft-threat toggle. The generator ranks available hulls using editable 1–5 ratings and assigns aircraft to compatible hangars.

03

Review the formation

Inspect front, middle, and back rows. Review aircraft capacity, unmet defensive roles and upgrade recommendations before making changes in the game.

04

Keep your work

Your settings auto-save in browser storage on this device. Export a JSON backup to transfer it to another browser; importing replaces the current local workspace.

Source notes & limitations

Initial ship scores and quantities are illustrative starter inputs. Actual unit characteristics, hull availability, variant modules, and combat balance can change. Check in-game values. Fighter sizes and hangar compatibility are simplified and should be checked for each host module.

Back up or restore your data

Blueprints are saved only in this browser. Export a backup before switching devices or clearing browser data.
